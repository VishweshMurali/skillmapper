# ML Engineer — Fenwick AI

## Overview

Fenwick AI builds retrieval-augmented copilots for customer support teams. We're hiring an ML Engineer to help scale our RAG pipeline and serving infrastructure as usage grows.

## Responsibilities

Own the retrieval and generation pipeline end to end, including chunking strategy, vector store design, and hybrid retrieval quality. Build and maintain an evaluation harness to track model-quality regressions across releases. Optimize inference latency and serving cost through caching, batching, and model-serving infrastructure choices. Collaborate with product on prompt design and failure-mode analysis.

## Requirements

At least 3 years of experience building and deploying machine learning systems in production. Hands-on experience with retrieval-augmented generation, including vector databases (e.g. ChromaDB, Pinecone, Weaviate) and hybrid (keyword + semantic) retrieval. Strong Python skills and experience with an ML framework (PyTorch preferred). Experience with LLM orchestration frameworks such as LangChain or LangGraph.

## Qualifications

Experience with RAG evaluation frameworks (e.g. RAGAS) is a strong plus. Experience with model-serving infrastructure (vLLM, TorchServe, ONNX Runtime) is a plus. Experience with MLOps tooling (MLflow, model versioning, canary rollout) is a plus. Contributions to open-source ML infrastructure projects are a plus.
