# Senior Backend Software Engineer — Northwind Payments

## Overview

Northwind Payments is hiring a Senior Backend Software Engineer to help scale our core ledger and payments-processing platform. You'll work on distributed, high-throughput services that process financial transactions with strict correctness and latency requirements.

## Responsibilities

Design and build backend services in Python or Go handling millions of transactions per day. Own the reliability of services you build, including participating in an on-call rotation and driving incident response. Collaborate with the data science team to expose low-latency APIs consumed by real-time fraud-scoring models. Mentor mid-level and junior engineers on system design and code quality.

## Requirements

At least 5 years of professional backend software engineering experience. Strong proficiency in Python or Go. Hands-on experience with distributed systems concepts: message queues (Kafka or similar), caching (Redis), and service-to-service communication (gRPC or REST). Experience with relational databases at scale (PostgreSQL preferred). Familiarity with containerized deployment (Docker, Kubernetes).

## Qualifications

Experience owning an on-call rotation and writing incident postmortems is a strong plus. AWS or GCP certification is a plus but not required. Experience with infrastructure-as-code (Terraform) is a plus. Prior experience in fintech or payments is a plus but not required.
