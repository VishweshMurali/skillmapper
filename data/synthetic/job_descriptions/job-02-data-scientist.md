# Data Scientist — Meridian Retail Analytics

## Overview

Meridian Retail Analytics is looking for a Data Scientist to join our personalization and retention team. You'll build models that directly influence customer-facing product decisions, from churn prediction to targeted campaigns.

## Responsibilities

Build, validate, and ship machine learning models (classification, regression, forecasting) into production. Design and analyze A/B tests, including choosing appropriate statistical methodology. Partner with engineering to productionize models via batch or real-time pipelines. Communicate model results and tradeoffs clearly to non-technical product stakeholders.

## Requirements

At least 4 years of experience as a data scientist or applied statistician. Strong proficiency in Python and SQL. Experience with a modern ML library (scikit-learn, LightGBM, XGBoost, or PyTorch). Solid grounding in statistics, including hypothesis testing and experimental design. Experience working with large-scale data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift).

## Qualifications

Experience with NLP or time-series forecasting is a strong plus. Experience building or maintaining a feature store is a plus. An advanced degree (M.S. or Ph.D.) in a quantitative field is a plus but not required. Experience with orchestration tools (Airflow) is a plus.
